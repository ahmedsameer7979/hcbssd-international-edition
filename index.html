01

Who we are

A Kuwaiti non-profit, public-benefit society founded in 2022. We empower groups under social care to strengthen their dignity and independence through vocational, psychological and social rehabilitation, leading to sustainable inclusion in society and the labour market. Our work is grounded in Constructive Recognition Theory (CRT), and our outcomes are read through the DREC Scale (Dignity · Recognition · Empowerment).

“Participation is not a seat at the table. It is recognition of the capacity to contribute.”

03

Our framework: from theory to practice

Constructive Recognition Theory starts from a shared human condition: every person is exposed to vulnerability. Exposure is a position, not a stigma attached to a particular group. The theory therefore assesses an institution's capacity to respond to vulnerability, rather than judging an individual as vulnerable. It draws on the recognition tradition (Honneth, Fraser, Levinas, Ricoeur, Fineman) in dialogue with Islamic social philosophy (al-Shāṭibī's maqāṣid, Ibn Khaldūn).

Recognition

Identify who needs support, and where, as one would for any member of the organisation.

Dignity

Respect each person's standing inside the organisation: work that fits their circumstances, not sympathy alone.

Empowerment

Tasks matched to abilities, not assigned to meet a quota; contributions counted as for any employee.
